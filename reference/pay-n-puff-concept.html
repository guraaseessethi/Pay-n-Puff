Before you board

Store rules

Ontario law sets these. We follow every one, in the store and at your door.

  1. 19+ to enter, buy or receive a delivery. No exceptions, including with a parent.
  2. Bring photo ID. We ask anyone who looks under 25, at the counter and at the door.
  3. No buying for someone underage. We'll refuse the sale and log it.
  4. Testing follows provincial rules: two people at a time, your own device or a disposable mouthpiece.
  5. Nicotine is highly addictive. Everything we sell is 20 mg/mL or less and carries the Ontario excise stamp.
